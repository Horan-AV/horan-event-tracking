Horan Event Tracking

Prep, stock & event tracking · Horan AV

This will install Horan Event Tracking in your Applications folder.

  • Your stock, jobs and clients are stored on this Mac. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • It opens in your web browser (Safari, Chrome or Firefox) but runs entirely on this Mac. No internet connection is needed.
  • A small background helper starts when you log in, so the app is always ready.

The first time it opens, your Mac may ask to install Apple’s free Command Line Tools. This is a one-off download of about 5–10 minutes. Click Install, then open Horan Event Tracking again when it has finished.
