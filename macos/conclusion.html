You’re all set

Horan Event Tracking is opening in your browser now.

  • To open it later: use Launchpad or Spotlight, or find it in Applications.
  • If your Mac offers to install the Command Line Tools, click Install Tools. When it finishes, open Horan Event Tracking again.
  • Moving from another computer? In the app, go to Settings → Your data → Restore from backup.

To remove the app, drag Horan Event Tracking from Applications to the Bin. Your data is kept in your Library folder, in Application Support ▸ Horan Event Tracking.
